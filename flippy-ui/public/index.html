Connect to n8n

Enter the address of your n8n instance on your network. The webhook path is filled in automatically.

Where do I find this?

It's the URL you use to open n8n in your browser, e.g. http://192.168.1.50:5678. You can also paste the full chat webhook URL from the Chat trigger node.

Make sure the Chat trigger in the Flippy workflow has “Make chat publicly available” switched on, and the workflow is Active.
